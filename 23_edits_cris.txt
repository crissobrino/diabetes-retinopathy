Changes vs attempt_21.ipynb:

- (C-4) EfficientNet-B4-NS planned as 5th FT backbone.
  tf_efficientnet_b4_ns (Noisy Student pretrained) via timm.
  Feature dim = 1792. Head: Dropout(0.5) -> Linear(1792, 1).
  Stage 1 unfreezes blocks[6] + conv_head + bn2.
  Stage 2 additionally unfreezes blocks[5].

  Scientific backing:
  APTOS 2019 Kaggle (fundus DR grading, ~3600 images, same task):
  top-10 solutions exclusively used EfficientNet-B3 to B7.
  1st place: EfficientNet-B5 at 456px. 2nd place: B4 at 380px.
  Noisy Student pretraining (Xie et al., NeurIPS 2020) outperforms
  standard ImageNet init on fine-grained transfer tasks.

- (C-5) Input resolution increased to 380 px for all backbones.
  Rescale(256)->RandomCrop(224) replaced with Rescale(416)->
  RandomCrop/CenterCrop(380). Higher resolution preserves fine vessel
  detail and microaneurysms (10-125 um diameter; at 224 px these are
  ~2 px wide, at 380 px they are ~3-4 px).

- (C-6) Rotation augmentation expanded from ±15° to ±180°.
  Fundus images are rotationally symmetric: the optic disc/macula
  relative orientation depends on camera angle and patient position.
  Graham (Kaggle DR 2015 winner) used full 360° rotation as the
  primary augmentation. The ±15° window discarded 92% of valid
  rotations.

Outcome: The four existing backbones (DenseNet121, DenseNet169,
  ResNeXt50, SE-ResNeXt50) were successfully retrained at 380px with
  ±180° rotation. EfficientNet-B4 training caused CUDA OOM even on a
  fresh kernel (14.56 GiB GPU fully saturated at batch=16, 380px).
  Gradient checkpointing and reduced batch sizes did not resolve it.
  C-5 and C-6 changes carried forward into attempt_24; C-4 dropped.

Results: not submitted — see attempt_24.
