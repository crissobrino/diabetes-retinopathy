7. second_attempt_tuned_cache.ipynb — Cache pipeline experiment (DISCARDED)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Changes vs second_attempt_tuned:
- Introduced CachedRetinopathyDataset class: loads pre-processed images from
  cache_224/ directory as numpy .npy files instead of raw JPEGs
- Cache creation pipeline: CropByEye -> Rescale(256) -> CenterCrop(224), saved
  once per image as a (3, 224, 224) float array
- Training augmentation at runtime: RandomHorizontalFlip + RandomRotation +
  ColorJitter + Normalize (applied to already-cropped 224x224 tensors)
- All other components identical to second_attempt_tuned: same architecture,
  WeightedRandomSampler, AdamW + CosineAnnealingLR, TTA, ensemble logic,
  hyperparameters

Motivation: Cache creation was meant to remove the CropByEye+Rescale overhead
from the training loop. Epoch time dropped from ~160s to ~28s (5x speedup),
which would allow faster iteration and more experiments.

Why it was discarded:
- The cache applies CenterCrop(224) at creation time, so RandomCrop(224) is
  never applied during training. This eliminates a key source of augmentation:
  the standard pipeline samples a different 224x224 crop from a 256x256 image
  at every epoch (32px of positional jitter in both axes per epoch).
- On a 2000-image dataset, this per-epoch crop variation acts as an effective
  regulariser. Removing it caused the models to converge faster to lower peaks:
    EfficientNet S1 best val AUC: 0.748 (epoch 3) vs 0.779 (epoch 14) in tuned
    CustomNetV2 best val AUC:     0.640 (epoch 4) vs 0.656 (epoch 15) in tuned
- CropByEye is also applied only once per image in the cache; in the standard
  pipeline it re-runs each epoch, providing slight variation in the crop boundary
  depending on image loading state.
- The 5x speed gain does not compensate for the consistent AUC drop across both
  models. The cache approach should not be used unless RandomCrop is preserved
  (e.g. cache at a larger size and crop at runtime).

Results: custom AUC 0.736 / 0.730, ft AUC 0.742 / 0.728 (public / private)
Regression of ~0.030 on ft and ~0.030 on custom vs second_attempt_tuned.

8. third_attempt.ipynb — Experiments G-1, G-2, G-3: anti-overfitting
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Motivation from log analysis of second_attempt_tuned:
- EfficientNet Stage 1 shows a train/val AUC gap of 0.178 at the best checkpoint
  (epoch 14: train 0.957, val 0.779). The model is memorising the 2000 training
  images. After epoch 14 train AUC keeps climbing to 0.980+ while val degrades.
  Stage 2 barely improves (+0.002 TTA) because it inherits an already-overfit model.
- CustomNetV2 best val was at epoch 15, patience=10 stopped at epoch 25. But with
  CosineAnnealingLR(T_max=50), epoch 25 is the LR trough of the first cycle.
  Epochs 22-25 all had near-zero LR, causing apparent stagnation. Early stopping
  fired right before the LR would restart for a second cycle.

Change G-1: TVRandomResizedCrop replaces Rescale(256) + RandomCrop(224)
  New transform class (same numpy->PIL->torchvision->numpy pattern as TVCenterCrop):
    TVRandomResizedCrop(size=224, scale=(0.65, 1.0), ratio=(0.9, 1.1))
  Training pipeline becomes:
    CropByEye(0.10,1) -> TVRandomResizedCrop(224) -> TVRandomHorizontalFlip
    -> TVRandomRotation(15deg) -> TVColorJitter -> ToTensor -> Normalize
  Val/test pipeline unchanged: CropByEye -> Rescale(256) -> CenterCrop(224)
    -> ToTensor -> Normalize

  Rationale: RandomResizedCrop combines scale jitter (crops 65-100% of the image
  area) with spatial position jitter in a single operation. The previous pipeline
  only provided spatial jitter (RandomCrop over 32px), so adding scale variation
  increases the effective diversity of each training image per epoch. Scale
  invariance is particularly useful for fundus images where the eye fills different
  fractions of the frame depending on the camera and field of view.

Change G-2: Label smoothing eps=0.05 for EfficientNet training only
  Added label_smoothing=0.0 parameter to train_model(). When > 0 and phase='train',
  targets are smoothed before the loss: labels_for_loss = labels * 0.95 + 0.025
  This turns hard {0, 1} targets into soft {0.025, 0.975} targets.
  Called as train_model(..., label_smoothing=0.05) for EfficientNet Stage 1 only.
  CustomNetV2 keeps label_smoothing=0.0 (default, no change).

  Rationale: With train AUC reaching 0.97 at the best checkpoint, the model is
  producing very confident logits for training examples. Label smoothing prevents
  the model from being penalised for any residual uncertainty on memorised examples,
  acting as a soft regulariser without changing the architecture or learning rate.
  eps=0.05 is conservative to avoid damaging the signal on a small dataset.
  Not applied to CustomNetV2 because it is not overfitting (val AUC > train AUC
  throughout training due to dropout and WeightedRandomSampler).

Change G-3: CustomNetV2 CosineAnnealingLR T_max=50 -> T_max=25
  scheduler_custom = lr_scheduler.CosineAnnealingLR(optimizer_custom, T_max=25)
  num_epochs and patience unchanged (50 epochs, patience=10).

  Rationale: With T_max=50, one cosine cycle spans the entire 50-epoch budget.
  At epoch 25 (the midpoint) the LR reaches its minimum (~0). Patience=10 counted
  10 epochs with near-zero LR and stopped at epoch 25, just before the LR would
  restart. With T_max=25, two full cycles fit within the 50-epoch budget. Patience=10
  now covers roughly 40% of a single cycle, so it is more likely to fire mid-cycle
  (at a meaningful LR) rather than at the trough. If the model improves in the
  second cycle, it will now be able to run it.
