CUSTOM MODEL DEVELOPMENT SUMMARY
Diabetic Retinopathy Detection — CUSTOM category only
(fifth_attempt and sixth_attempt excluded as invalid.)
===========================================================================


CHRONOLOGICAL EVOLUTION OF CUSTOM APPROACHES
=============================================

1. BASELINE: first_attempt.ipynb
   Model:       CustomNet — 4 conv blocks with 5×5 filters (no padding), flatten
                64×10×10=6400, FC: 6400→120→84→Dropout(0.5)→1
   Training:    SGD + StepLR; no BenGraham; no augmentation beyond basic flips
   Result:      0.570 pub / 0.597 priv
   Notes:       Establishes the floor. Limited by shallow architecture and no
                preprocessing specific to fundus images.

--

2. attempt_with_b.ipynb  (Series B)
   Changes:     Switched CustomNet optimizer to Adam + CosineAnnealingLR
   Result:      0.558 pub / 0.566 priv
   Notes:       Slight regression. Adam+cosine alone does not compensate for
                architectural limitations of the original CustomNet.

--

3. attempt_with_a_b.ipynb  (Series A+B)
   Changes:     Added CLAHE preprocessing on top of Series B
   Result:      0.544 pub / 0.562 priv
   Notes:       CLAHE corrupts the intensity distribution that CropByEye relies
                on to detect the eye boundary → incorrect crops → performance
                drop. CLAHE is permanently banned from the pipeline.

--

4. attempt_with_c.ipynb  (Series C)
   Changes:     EfficientNet-B0 introduced for the FT track. CustomNet unchanged.
   Result:      0.561 pub / 0.579 priv  (custom track, CustomNet only)
   Notes:       No meaningful custom improvement; FT track improved significantly.

--

5. second_attempt.ipynb  (Series D–F)
   Model:       CustomNetV2 — 5 blocks, 3×3 same-padding (channels 3→32→64→128
                →256→256, bias=False), Global Average Pooling → 256-dim,
                FC: Dropout(0.2)→128→ReLU→Dropout(0.4)→1. ~1M params.
   Training:    BenGraham preprocessing introduced (sigmaX=10, circular mask,
                uint8 guard NOT yet applied → gray-image bug still latent)
   Result:      0.744 pub / 0.731 priv
   Notes:       Major jump (+0.17 pub). GAP + deeper channels outperform the
                original flatten approach. CustomNetV2 becomes the backbone for
                all subsequent custom models.

--

6. second_attempt_tuned.ipynb
   Changes:     WeightedRandomSampler added (inverse-frequency weights for 73/27
                class imbalance)
   Result:      0.766 pub / 0.743 priv
   Notes:       +0.022 pub gain purely from rebalancing. Sampler remains in all
                subsequent runs.

--

7. second_attempt_tuned_cache.ipynb
   Changes:     Pre-cached 224px images to disk to speed up training
   Result:      0.736 pub / 0.730 priv  (−0.030 pub regression)
   Notes:       Cache removes on-the-fly RandomCrop, eliminating per-epoch
                spatial variation that acts as the primary regulariser on a
                2000-image dataset. Speed gain not worth the AUC cost.
                Cache pipeline permanently abandoned.

--

8. third_attempt.ipynb  (Series G)
   Changes:     TVRandomResizedCrop replacing random crop, label smoothing
                ε=0.05, T_max=25 for cosine schedule
   Result:      0.762 pub / 0.742 priv
   Notes:       Marginal gain vs tuned baseline. ResizedCrop later reverted
                (less control over scale); label smoothing not retained.

--

9. fourth_attempt.ipynb  (Series H) — CATASTROPHIC FAILURE
   Changes:     BenGraham preprocessing re-applied but missing uint8 dtype guard
   Result:      0.541 pub / 0.557 priv  (−0.225 pub regression)
   Root cause:  Without the guard, numpy clips uint8 values to {0,1} when
                computing 4*img − 4*GaussianBlur + 128. Every pixel becomes
                0 or 128, producing uniform gray images. The model trains on
                noise. Fix: add `if image.dtype == np.uint8` before any
                float arithmetic on the image. Applied from fifth_attempt onward.

--

10. seventh_attempt.ipynb  (Series J)
    Model:      SE-CustomNetV2 — adds SEBlock(r=8) after Conv-BN-ReLU in every
                one of the 5 blocks (~22k extra params). 2-seed ensemble (42+123).
    Changes:    8-pass TTA (added rot90 variants), DenseNet Stage 3 unfreeze
                attempted (custom is SE-V2 here)
    Result:     0.756 pub / 0.762 priv
    Notes:      SE attention actually regressed vs plain V2 (~0.770 before it
                was invalidated). 8-TTA with rot90 hurts — distribution shift.
                Reverted to 4-TTA. SE modules add complexity that overfits
                on 2000 training images.

--

11. eighth_attempt.ipynb  (Series K)
    Models:     SE-CustomNetV2 (seed=42) + CustomNetV3 (seed=42), 2-model ensemble
    CustomNetV3: same channel progression as V2 but with residual connections —
                ResBlock = Conv3×3→BN + 1×1-projection-skip → ReLU → MaxPool.
                ~1.05M params.
    Changes:    4-TTA restored (removed rot90), cudnn.deterministic fix
    Result:     0.756 pub / 0.764 priv
    Notes:      Marginal gain over seventh. Both SE attention and residuals add
                complexity that barely moves the needle on this dataset size.
                Grid-searched ensemble weight w∈{0.1..0.9} on 500-sample val
                (later shown to overfit).

--

12. tenth_attempt.ipynb  (Series M)
    Models:     4-architecture uniform ensemble: CustomNetV2 + SE-CustomNetV2 +
                CustomNetV3 + SECustomNetV3 (new), all seed=42.
    SECustomNetV3: SEResBlock = Conv3×3→BN + 1×1-skip → ReLU → SEBlock(r=8)
                → MaxPool. Combines V3 residuals with SE attention. ~1.09M params.
    Changes:    Single seed (no multi-seed diversity); uniform mean ensemble
                (no val-based weight grid search)
    Result:     0.760 pub / 0.773 priv
    Notes:      SE-V2 alone scored ~0.52 TTA-4 val AUC — failed to converge
                entirely. Diversity across 4 architectures partially compensates
                but private AUC is lower than ninth_attempt's 3-seed ensemble.
                Uniform mean is optimal when model diversity is architectural;
                val-based weight search overfits on 500 samples.

--

13. tenth_extension_attempt.ipynb  (Series M-ext)  — INFERENCE ONLY
    Models:     3-model ensemble from tenth_attempt checkpoints: V2 + V3 + SE-V3
                SE-V2 excluded (val AUC ~0.52 confirmed in tenth_attempt)
    Result:     0.760 pub / 0.772 priv
    Notes:      Essentially matches tenth_attempt. Removing the broken SE-V2
                does not meaningfully change the ensemble score because its
                contribution was already diluted by uniform mean averaging.

--

14. twelfth_attempt.ipynb  (Series N2)  — NOT SUBMITTED
    Model:      Plain CustomNetV2, 3-seed (42, 123, 456) — same as ninth_attempt
                but at 380px resolution (Rescale 416→RandomCrop 380) with
                ±180° rotation augmentation
    Result:     3-seed TTA-4 ensemble val AUC = 0.530  (not submitted)
    Notes:      Confirmed poor convergence. Higher resolution + stronger rotation
                appear to harm the small custom model. The 380px pipeline was
                effective for fine-tuned backbones (pretrained on ImageNet at
                high resolution) but this same configuration does not transfer
                to training from scratch on 2000 images.


===========================================================================
FINAL CUSTOM VERSION: ninth_attempt.ipynb  (best by private AUC: 0.757 pub / 0.780 priv)
===========================================================================

Rationale for ninth_attempt as best:
  Public AUC  (first 500 test images):  0.757
  Private AUC (second 500 test images): 0.780
  tenth_attempt private AUC is 0.773 — seventh's multi-seed approach
  generalises better to unseen data than fourth architecture's single-seed ensemble.

--- Architecture ---

CustomNetV2 (plain, no SE, no residuals):
  Block structure:  5 × [Conv2d(3×3, same-padding, bias=False) → BN → ReLU → MaxPool2d(2)]
  Channel sizes:    3 → 32 → 64 → 128 → 256 → 256
  Spatial path:     224 → 112 → 56 → 28 → 14 → 7
  Head:             GlobalAveragePool → Flatten(256) → Dropout(0.2) → Linear(256,128)
                    → ReLU → Dropout(0.4) → Linear(128,1)
  Parameters:       ~1.01M  (no pretrained weights)

--- Data Pipeline (224px) ---

Training:
  CropByEye(threshold=0.10, border=1)
  → BenGraham(sigmaX=10)          [4*img − 4*GaussianBlur(img,10) + 128; uint8 guard + circular mask]
  → Rescale(256)
  → TVRandomHorizontalFlip(p=0.5)
  → TVRandomVerticalFlip(p=0.5)   [(L-3) added to match TTA vflip pass]
  → TVRandomRotation(15°)
  → TVColorJitter(brightness=0.3, contrast=0.3, saturation=0.2, hue=0.05)
  → RandomCrop(224)
  → ToTensor → Normalize(mean=[0.485,0.456,0.406], std=[0.229,0.224,0.225])

Validation / Test:
  CropByEye → BenGraham → Rescale(256) → CenterCrop(224) → ToTensor → Normalize

--- Training Configuration ---

Seeds:              42, 123, 456 (three independent runs of the same architecture)
Optimizer:          AdamW(lr=1e-3, weight_decay=5e-3)
Scheduler:          CosineAnnealingLR(T_max=50)
Loss:               BCEWithLogitsLoss(pos_weight = n_neg/n_pos ≈ 2.7)
Epochs:             50 max, patience=10 (early stopping on val AUC)
Label smoothing:    0.0
Batch sizes:        64 train / 256 val (num_workers=0, Windows)
Sampler:            WeightedRandomSampler (inverse-frequency class weights)
cudnn:              deterministic=True, benchmark=False

--- Ensemble ---

Method:   Uniform arithmetic mean of sigmoid outputs from the 3 trained models
          (no val-based weight grid search — overfits on 500-sample val set;
          equal weights are asymptotically optimal for same-architecture models)
TTA:      4-pass — original + hflip + vflip + rot180; mean of sigmoid outputs
          (rot90 TTA excluded — causes distribution shift; verified in K-1)
Diversity source: stochastic weight initialisation, batch ordering, augmentation
                  (not architectural — avoids overfitting on 2000 training images)

--- Key Design Decisions (L-series changes) ---

(L-1)  Revert to plain CustomNetV2 — SE blocks and residuals both regressed vs
       plain V2. Root cause: added complexity overfits on 2000-image dataset.
(L-2)  3-seed ensemble replaces 2-arch ensemble — more reliable diversity without
       architectural complexity risk.
(L-3)  TVRandomVerticalFlip added to training augmentation — aligns training
       distribution with TTA-4 vflip pass, which was present at inference but
       absent during training in earlier attempts.


===========================================================================
FAILED CUSTOM APPROACHES
===========================================================================

The following directions were explored and abandoned. Each entry explains
what was tried, what happened, and the root cause where known.

--- Architecture Complexity ---

1. SE attention (SEBlock r=8) on all blocks — seventh_attempt, eighth_attempt, tenth_attempt
   What:     Squeeze-and-Excitation blocks added after Conv-BN-ReLU in every
             CustomNetV2 block. (~22k extra parameters)
   Outcome:  Val AUC regressed vs plain V2. SE-V2 alone in tenth_attempt reached
             only ~0.52 val AUC (effectively random).
   Why it failed: Dataset too small (2000 images) to learn meaningful channel
             attention. Extra parameters increase overfitting risk without
             the benefit that comes from large-scale ImageNet pretraining.

2. Residual connections (CustomNetV3) — eighth_attempt, tenth_attempt
   What:     Each block replaced with ResBlock = Conv3×3→BN + 1×1-projection
             skip → ReLU → MaxPool. Same parameter count as V2 (~1.05M).
   Outcome:  Marginal improvement at best (+0.002 over V2 single seed).
             Combined SE+residual (SECustomNetV3) in tenth_attempt brought
             ensemble private AUC below ninth_attempt's pure V2 ensemble.
   Why it failed: Skip connections are most useful for very deep networks where
             gradient flow is a bottleneck. At 5 blocks, plain V2 already
             trains without vanishing gradient issues.

3. Partial SE (attempt_20) — SE on blocks 3–5 only (reduction=16)
   What:     Applied SE attention only to the deeper layers, hoping to avoid
             overfitting in early feature extractors.
   Outcome:  ~0.72 val AUC — even worse than full SE blocks. Not submitted.
   Why it failed: Inconsistency between early (plain) and late (SE) blocks does
             not resolve the fundamental overfitting issue.

--- Preprocessing Mistakes ---

4. CLAHE preprocessing — attempt_with_a_b
   What:     Contrast-limited adaptive histogram equalisation applied before
             the standard pipeline.
   Outcome:  0.544 pub (−0.022 regression from Series B baseline)
   Why it failed: CLAHE alters the global intensity distribution. CropByEye
             uses a fixed threshold (0.10) to detect the eye boundary from
             raw pixel intensity. After CLAHE, background pixels may exceed
             the threshold, corrupting the crop.

5. BenGraham without uint8 guard — fourth_attempt (catastrophic)
   What:     BenGraham formula applied directly to uint8 image arrays without
             first casting to float.
   Outcome:  0.541 pub — catastrophic collapse. Model trained on uniform gray images.
   Why it failed: numpy clips uint8 arithmetic to [0,255]; operations like
             4*img can silently overflow. Values collapse to {0,128}. The
             resulting training images are near-uniform gray patches.

--- Data Pipeline Modifications ---

6. Pre-cached image pipeline — second_attempt_tuned_cache
   What:     Images preprocessed and saved to disk at 224px; DataLoader reads
             cached tensors directly, skipping online augmentation.
   Outcome:  0.736 pub (−0.030 regression from tuned baseline)
   Why it failed: Cache eliminates RandomCrop as a per-epoch augmentation.
             On a 2000-image training set, spatial crop variation is the
             primary source of regularisation. Without it the model overfits.

7. rot90 variants in TTA (8-pass TTA) — seventh_attempt
   What:     Extended TTA from 4 passes (orig+hflip+vflip+rot180) to 8 passes
             by adding rot90 and rot270 variants.
   Outcome:  AUC decreased for both custom and FT models.
   Why it failed: Fundus images are radially symmetric but the BenGraham +
             rectangular crop pipeline is not rotation-invariant at 90°.
             rot90 images fall outside the training distribution, producing
             miscalibrated predictions that reduce mean ensemble quality.

8. Higher resolution for custom model — twelfth_attempt (N2-series)
   What:     Exact ninth_attempt setup (3-seed CustomNetV2) but at 380px
             (Rescale 416 → RandomCrop 380) with ±180° rotation augmentation.
   Outcome:  3-seed TTA-4 val AUC = 0.530 — effectively random. Not submitted.
   Why it failed: 380px quadruples spatial computation. The custom model's
             5×MaxPool2d(2) path assumes 224px input (7×7 feature map before
             GAP). At 380px the GAP aggregates over an 11×11 map — a different
             receptive field regime. Additionally, ±180° rotation works for
             pretrained backbones that are already robust to rotation (trained
             on diverse ImageNet) but training from scratch at this difficulty
             level with 2000 images causes the model to fail to converge.

--- DenseNet Stage 3 Unfreeze (fine-tuning but relevant context) ---

9. DenseNet Stage 3 (denseblock2+transition2) unfreeze — seventh_attempt
   What:     Unfroze deeper DenseNet layers beyond Stage 2.
   Outcome:  FT AUC regressed from 0.781 to 0.768.
   Why it failed: Over-parameterises the fine-tuning with 2000 training images.
             Stages 1+2 already achieve near-optimal AUC; Stage 3 simply overfits.
