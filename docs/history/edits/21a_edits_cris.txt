  vs attempt_21 full ensemble: TBD (ensemble not yet submitted).

Analysis:
  ResNeXt50 alone outperforms the sixth_attempt DenseNet121 pipeline
  by ~0.9pp on the public leaderboard, confirming that the resnext
  backbone is a stronger feature extractor for this dataset than
  DenseNet121. The gap between val AUC (0.8063) and test AUC (0.790)
  suggests mild overfitting or distribution shift on the held-out
  test set (~1.6pp gap vs ~0pp gap for sixth_attempt).
