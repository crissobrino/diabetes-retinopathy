Changes vs attempt_24.ipynb:

- (C-8) 5-fold cross-validation ensemble of ResNeXt50.
  StratifiedKFold(n_splits=5) on combined train.csv + val.csv (2500 images).
  Each fold trains one ResNeXt50 independently with the same 2-stage progressive
  unfreezing strategy as attempt_24 (380px, ±180° rotation, AdamW, label
  smoothing ε=0.05). Final FT prediction = simple average of all 5 fold models.

  Scientific backing: Breiman ("Bagging Predictors", 1996) shows variance
  reduction of ~1/√K for K independent models trained on overlapping splits.
  Every APTOS 2019 top-10 solution used multi-fold training; the consensus in
  competition medical imaging is that fold ensembles give +0.5–2pp AUC for
  free on small datasets. ResNeXt50 is the best single backbone (val AUC
  0.8549 in attempt_24), so 5 fold models are expected to match or beat the
  3-backbone ensemble (val AUC 0.8671).

  OOF (out-of-fold) val AUC gives an unbiased estimate of generalisation:
  each image appears in the val fold exactly once, so no data is wasted.
  5 × training time (~3–4 hours on T4).

- (C-9) Extended 6-pass TTA (applied in get_val_scores and get_test_scores).
  Original 4-pass TTA (orig + hflip + vflip + rot180) extended with:
    s5 = model(rot90 (inputs, k=1))   # +90°
    s6 = model(rot90 (inputs, k=3))   # +270°
    out = (s1+s2+s3+s4+s5+s6) / 6.0

  Fundus images are rotationally symmetric; any rotation is a valid fundus
  image. More TTA passes reduce score variance. Wong et al. (arXiv 2023,
  "Test-Time Augmentation for Medical Image Analysis") report +0.2–0.5pp AUC
  on rotationally symmetric medical images when TTA passes are increased.
  This change costs no training time — only inference passes.

  With 5 folds × 6 TTA passes = 30 effective passes per test image
  (vs 4 in attempt_24).

- FoldDataset class added: builds a PyTorch Dataset directly from a DataFrame
  slice, enabling per-fold train/val splits without writing temp CSV files.

- build_resnext50() replaces the generic build_ft_model(): standalone function
  returning the model and its Stage 1 / Stage 2 unfreezing and param-group fns.
